## Caveats
- PIB never names the GREAT start-ups; only project titles and counts. Company names are on nttm.texmin.gov.in.
- Title-based filtering on the index; a release whose title omits "award", "start-up", "TDB", "textile" could be missed.
- Not checked: TDB award winners for 2023 and 2025 beyond what the National Technology Day releases carry.
