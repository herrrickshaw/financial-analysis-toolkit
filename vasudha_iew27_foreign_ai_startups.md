# Foreign AI-tool startups suited to MC2 / Vasudha – IEW'27 (Overseas Upstream Startup Challenge)

Compiled 2026-11-02 from the FIPI notice (dated 19 Aug 2026) and open web sources. Verify incorporation dates and funding stage against each company's own records before shortlisting or inviting.

## The event in one paragraph

FIPI (Federation of Indian Petroleum Industry), under the Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas, runs an overseas-startup challenge alongside India Energy Week 2027 (28–31 Jan 2027, Convention Centre, New Town, Kolkata). The challenge was renamed from "Vasudha" to "MC2 – IEW'27 Oil & Gas Upstream Sector Overseas Startup Challenge". A jury picks two startups; each gets flights for two, hotel 27 Jan–1 Feb, a furnished stall, CXO meetings with Indian upstream companies, and possible felicitation by the Minister at the closing ceremony.

Source: https://www.fipi.org.in/Vasudha-IEW%2027.php and the notice PDF `Upload/Notice Inviting Entries - IEW'27_5Oct26.pdf`.

## Eligibility and deadline (from the notice)

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Who | Startups incorporated outside India (with or without an Indian office). Indian startups go to the separate MC2 / Avinya challenge. |
| Age | Max 10 years from incorporation as of 1 Jan 2027 (so incorporated on/after ~1 Jan 2017) |
| Stage | Ideation or early stage: bootstrapped, pre-seed, seed, Series A, B or C |
| Not eligible | Entities formed by splitting or reconstructing an existing business |
| Tracks (pick exactly one) | (a) Deepwater & ultra-deepwater exploration; (b) AI & digital in exploration, production and reservoir management; (c) CCUS and CO2-EOR; (d) Geothermal exploration and reuse of existing well infrastructure |
| Entry deadline | 15 Nov 2026, 23:59 IST (query window and Zoom clarification already passed) |
| Documents | 500-word description, startup registration proof, business plan, IP agreements if any |
| Apply | Google Form linked from the FIPI page; or email karan@fipi.org.in (≤20 MB) |

## Past winners (benchmarks for what the jury likes)

- IEW'26 (Goa): Senergetics, Netherlands — AI + photonic sensors for corrosion-under-insulation monitoring. Runner-up per PIB document: Resermine Inc., USA (reservoir AI).
- IEW'25 (New Delhi): Latin Energy Partners, Paraguay (winner); Ultrasound Process Consulting, USA (runner-up).

Takeaway: small, hardware-plus-AI or physics-plus-ML teams from non-obvious geographies have won. Pure software is fine but needs a clear upstream use case.

## Strong fit but probably INELIGIBLE on the 10-year rule (incorporated before 2017)

Ambyint (Canada, 2014), Tachyus (USA, 2013), Novi Labs (USA, 2014), Earth Science Analytics (Norway, 2016), Turbulent Flux (Norway, 2016), Bluware (USA, acquired by CMG), Geoteric (UK), Eliis (France), OpenGoSim (UK, 2014), Belmont Technology (USA, ~2017, borderline). Worth contacting only if FIPI relaxes the age rule or if they have a qualifying spin-out.

## Recommended outreach order

1. Zanskar — biggest AI-geothermal story, within Series C.
2. Applied Computing — freshest, best-funded upstream AI model; UK.
3. Terra AI — one company that can credibly pitch tracks (b), (c) or (d).
4. Ignis Energy — AI exploration with field proof in Türkiye.
5. Gradient Geothermal — perfect match for the "existing wells" wording.
6. SubsurfaceAI, Resermine, Cortex — smaller, verify age/stage first.
