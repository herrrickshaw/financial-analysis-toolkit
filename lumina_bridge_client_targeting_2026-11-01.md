## 2. How to estimate savings (existing model, illustrative)
Net saving = cheaper US$/AED finance + tax on arm's-length margin booked in UAE (9% vs 25.17%) + fewer FX conversions + logistics/stock - cost of structure.
Assumptions in the model: 90 receivable days, 70% financed, rupee 9.0% vs US$/AED 6.25%, 3% UAE margin, 0.25% FX, 0.5% logistics.
| Gulf sales | Structure | Net saving/yr | Break-even sales |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rs 25 cr | IFZA, founder-run | Rs 0.28 cr | ~Rs 8.5 cr |
| Rs 100 cr | DMCC + 1 manager | Rs 0.86 cr | ~Rs 50 cr |
| Rs 500 cr | DIFC SPV + DMCC + 2 staff | Rs 6.61 cr | ~Rs 113 cr |
Per-client inputs to collect: Gulf sales, receivable days, current financing (PCFC already in US$ cuts the finance saving by 40-50%), margin, profit-making or not, whether it has real UAE substance (staff, office, decisions).
Caveats the model itself states: tax saving needs UAE substance (Indian POEM/GAAR/transfer pricing); loss-makers get no tax saving; EU parents lose it to CFC rules (Germany); unhedged rupee earners take an FX bet (a ~5% rupee fall wipes out the benefit); new-sales upside not counted.
Government-support layer to add to a client's savings: EPM interest subvention, factoring and credit guarantee (Section 3). Count only what the client is eligible for.

## 3. Government export-promotion priorities (PIB, verified by curl)
Export Promotion Mission, Cabinet 12 Nov 2025, outlay Rs 25,060 cr, FY26-FY31, implemented by DGFT (PIB PRID 2189381, 2199733):
- Priority sectors: textiles, leather, gems and jewellery, engineering goods, marine products (tariff-hit); MSMEs, first-time exporters, non-traditional districts.
- Niryat Protsahan: interest subvention, export factoring, collateral guarantees, credit enhancement for new markets.
- Niryat Disha: compliance and certification, branding, packaging, trade fairs, warehousing, inland transport, trade intelligence.
- Rs 20,000 cr credit guarantee scheme plus RBI relief measures (PRID 2199733).
- India-GCC FTA: terms of reference 5 Feb 2026, talks launched 24 Feb 2026; phased with early harvest. Focus areas reported: energy, petrochemicals, gems and jewellery, food, engineering goods, pharma. Talks reportedly slowed by the West Asia crisis; verify current round dates.
- India-EU FTA: concluded late Jan 2026 per a secondary source; signing/entry into force not confirmed.
I did not find a PIB "task force" release. Check the Commerce Ministry and DGFT notifications for any named task force.
