1 · Six missions now set the research agenda

Most of the "new" topics at IITs and NITs since 2024 trace back to a handful of central missions. Each one routes money through a few host institutes, so knowing the mission tells you which campus, and often which professor, holds the grant.

Read the ANRF numbers carefully. ANRF's 2025-26 allocation was ₹1,506 cr but only ₹603 cr was used (a parliamentary panel put utilisation at 61%). The 2026-27 allocation jumps to ₹2,000 cr. Expect a surge of calls (ACE, IRG, MAHA Drones, MAHA Water, AI for Science) closing through late 2026, which is good timing for anyone preparing proposals.

6 · Industry partnerships by institute

Three mechanisms dominate: master research agreements with one large company, company-funded Centres of Excellence on campus, and industry seats inside mission consortia (MAHA-EV requires them). CSR money is now a major channel at IIT Bombay and IIT Madras.

IIT Madras

  • Industry-funded projects rose from 501 (2015-16) to 982 (2025-26); industry funding ₹966 cr in 2025-26.
  • Named partners: Walmart, FedEx, Hyundai, multiple PSUs; Technology Summit for CEOs in Delhi, May 2026.
  • Research Park CoEs: Advanced Automotive Research, Battery Engineering & EVs, Wireless Technology, Railway Research, Kotak Save Energy Mission, Road Safety, Lab-Grown Diamond, International Centre for Clean Water.
  • C-DOT co-hosts the quantum communication hub on campus.

IIT Bombay

  • IRCC secured $73.4 M research funding and filed 305 IP applications in FY26.
  • Tata Power Master Research Agreement (Aug 2026): smart grids, storage, e-mobility, AI for power systems, carbon capture.
  • ASPIRE Research Park: 35 resident and 25 associate companies, 40+ active collaborations; 260+ long-term CSR partnerships funding 300+ projects.
  • MAHA-EV industry partners include Tata Motors, TVS, Jio-bp, John Deere, Mitsubishi Electric, Permanent Magnets.

IIT Delhi

  • Industry Membership Program launched May 2026 through the Corporate Relations Office.

  • DRDO Industry-Academia CoE transferred the ABHED bulletproof jacket to three firms.

How to use this for outreach. For a research tie-up, go through the hub's Section-8 company (Qmet, Samgnya, QMD, TiHAN, IHFC, BharatGen): they have CEOs and open calls, and they can contract faster than the institute. For a sponsored project, the institute R&D office is the gate: IRCC at IIT Bombay, ICSR/CSR at IIT Madras, FITT/CRO at IIT Delhi, DORA at IIT Kanpur, Dean R&C at the NITs. For a startup, the incubator (IITMIC, SINE, FSID) is a better first door than a department.
