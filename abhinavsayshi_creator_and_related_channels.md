**What the channel is.** Hinglish, face-to-camera Shorts (~60s) profiling Indian companies — mostly family-owned, unlisted or mid-cap manufacturers and the people behind them ("hidden champions": helmets, gears, water tanks, hazelnut suppliers to Ferrero, frozen-paratha exporters, McDonald's India JV partner Smita Jatia). The positioning is explicitly "family business / old money insider", born from his own position in a Karnal business family. Started on a whim (Instagram first, YouTube mirror later); he says he picked the niche because design/AI/startup content was saturated while "family business and old money felt like an untouched category". He claims to avoid "false numbers or false narratives".

**Content formula (inferred from titles):** "INDIA ka ___ EMPIRE", "The ___ behind ___ India", "X depends on THIS Indian company" — one company per Short, emoji-heavy titles, 🇮🇳 flag, surprise hook.
